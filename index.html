    I'm Hamdan. I study data science and AI at United Arab Emirates University, and I like software that is finished.

    Finished means it has a live address, it works on a phone, and you can try it without asking me first. Every world above is one of those.

    Most of what I make checks a claim against its source: a register, a price page, a permit office, my own university portal. When a tool of mine can't prove something, it says so and leaves the gap open. I would rather show a blank than a guess.
